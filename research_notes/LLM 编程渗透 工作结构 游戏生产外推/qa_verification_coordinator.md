Implications for the report (game side):
- The cleanest worker-level adoption series (GDC personal use) is **flat at ~36% since 2025**, while negative sentiment rose 18% → 30% → 52%. Vendor surveys (Unity 95%, Google/Harris 90%) measure something broader. Present the layers separately.
- Player-facing AI (Steam disclosures) is growing fast in **count** (10.9% → 30.8% of releases) but lags in **sales share** (~10–27%). This is consistent with "volume ≠ value", subject to selection effects.
- China claims (>99% adoption; 86.36% production-side application; 三七 >80% 2D efficiency) are self-reported or industry-authored. Label them as such.

Implications for the report (coding side):
- The adoption layers diverge: **breadth** is saturated (~84–90% use), **daily use** is around half, and **agent delegation** is rising fast. Measures of the latter include Claude Code 18% → 39% of professional devs in six months (JetBrains) and >50% of devs writing <20% of their code by hand.
- "AI share of code" claims now range from **~29% (independent classifier, end-2024)** to **"most"/~75–80% (Google, Tencent, Anthropic self-reports, 2026)**. The definitions differ, and only the Science estimate is independent.
- Value capture is visible at the vendor level: Cursor >$4B ARR and a $60B acquisition; Claude Code $2.5B by Feb 2026.
