Implications for the report (game side):
- The cleanest worker-level adoption series (GDC personal use) is **flat at ~36% since 2025**, while negative sentiment rose 18% → 30% → 52%. Vendor surveys (Unity 95%, Google/Harris 90%) measure something broader. Present the layers separately.
- Player-facing AI (Steam disclosures) is growing fast in **count** (10.9% → 30.8% of releases) but lags in **sales share** (~10–27%). This is consistent with "volume ≠ value", subject to selection effects.
- China claims (>99% adoption; 86.36% production-side application; 三七 >80% 2D efficiency) are self-reported or industry-authored. Label them as such.
